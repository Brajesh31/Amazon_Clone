# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, Brajesh Kumar (`@brajesh31`). Reports will be reviewed and handled in good faith. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
