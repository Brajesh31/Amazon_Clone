# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Amazon Clone repository is an educational project. Its community should remain welcoming to people learning frontend development, experienced contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and visitors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participation in project spaces is expected to be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Use considerate and inclusive language.
- Critique code, documentation, and decisions without attacking people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and practical reasoning.
- Welcome questions from beginners and avoid gatekeeping.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and proportionate.
- Respect review decisions and the time of maintainers.
- Keep discussions relevant to the repository and its educational scope.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct inaccurate claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination or degrading comments based on personal characteristics.
- Personal attacks, insults, humiliation, or deliberate hostility.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission.
- Publishing credentials, private messages, or confidential information.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Misrepresenting another person's work or falsely claiming project capabilities.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, documentation contributions, and other project spaces used for collaboration.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, Brajesh Kumar, through the contact method available on the GitHub profile [@brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31). Do not use a public issue to expose sensitive personal details.

A report should include the relevant context, links or dates where available, and any immediate safety concern. Share only information necessary to understand the report.

## Review and enforcement

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Depending on the circumstances, project maintainers may:
1. Ask participants to stop or correct the behaviour.
2. Remove inappropriate content.
3. Decline or close contributions that violate the standard.
4. Restrict participation in repository spaces.
5. Escalate serious threats or unlawful conduct through appropriate channels.

Actions should be proportionate to the conduct and taken with attention to participant safety.

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers should communicate expectations clearly, review reports privately, avoid retaliatory conduct, and explain moderation decisions when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.
